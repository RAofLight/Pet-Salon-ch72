Who We Are:

Ruff, Bark, Meow! Paws-n-Treats is a lisenced and accredited Pet Service Business. We have been operating since 2012. Our mission is to provide your pets with a relaxing and positive experience. At Paws-n-Treats, we handle your pets with the utmost care and are intentional with every service that we provide. Here, we take a different approach with your pets, using products that are natural and organic. We also provide a TCM massage for every pet that comes in and after they are done with their service, we spritz them with an essential oil of you choosing and give you a goodie bag to take home for them. Most pets get stressed or hate the groomery, we want your pet to love coming back to us and leave feeling like their best selves!!!
